No. Field Materials runs on top of Viewpoint Vista, Spectrum, Foundation, Sage, CMiC, QuickBooks, Procore, and more. It straightens out purchasing, receiving, and the 3-way match before anything lands in accounting, so the system you already pay for does more, not less.

How quickly can we be running, and is it all or nothing?+

It goes live in pieces. Most teams switch on invoice capture or PO entry first, watch the hours come back inside a couple of weeks, then layer in matching, RFQs, and inventory whenever they are ready. There is no year-long rollout.

Will the crews in the field actually adopt it?+

The office gets the payoff even when the field barely touches it. And for crews that want to do more, the mobile app supports it, so there is nothing to force and nothing to train.
