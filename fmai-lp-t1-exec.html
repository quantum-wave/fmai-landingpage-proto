Good to know

The questions every owner asks.

Do we have to replace our current ERP?+

Not at all. Field Materials sits on top of the accounting system you already run, cleaning up procurement and AP before anything reaches it. It connects with Sage 100, Sage 300 CRE, Sage Intacct, Foundation, Viewpoint Vista, Viewpoint Spectrum, CMiC, QuickBooks, and Procore, so the platform you've already invested in does more, not less.

Will crews in the field actually adopt it?+

They do, because it's easier than what they do today. Ordering takes a few taps, or a quick photo of the same handwritten note they already jot down, and the AI reads it even when it's in Spanish or stained and crumpled from the job site. No retraining required. But you don't need them to. The AP automation, 3-way match, and ERP sync deliver the value whether or not a single crew member ever opens the app, so field use is a bonus on top, never a precondition.

How fast can we get up and running?+

You roll it out one module at a time, in whatever order suits you. Many teams switch on AP first because it pays off quickest, then layer in the rest when they're ready. There's no all-at-once, multi-month implementation to survive.

What should we expect from the demo?+

A focused half hour on your own process, with no pressure to buy. You'll watch how your quotes, POs, delivery slips, receipts, and invoices would move through the platform, and most leaders come away understanding where time and margin are slipping today, which is valuable on its own no matter what you decide next.
